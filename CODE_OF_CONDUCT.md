# Code of conduct

RyProx aims to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Give and take feedback on code, not on people.
- Respect differing views and experiences.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer via the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or ban participants who break these rules.

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and chat.
